A little about me

Technical mind.
Creative instinct.

I'm a Computer Science student at the Federal University of Lafia with a growing focus on software engineering, product development, and human-centred design.

I enjoy working across the full journey: understanding a problem, shaping the experience, building the product, and communicating why it matters. Alongside software, visual design and creative production help me see problems from a different angle.

I'm especially interested in practical technology — tools that make everyday work easier, digital payments more intuitive, and information easier to access. Long term, I'm exploring cloud engineering and the connection between software, hardware, and intelligent devices.
